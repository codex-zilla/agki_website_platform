The Challenge Barriers to Quality Healthcare

Millions of people still face interconnected barriers that make essential healthcare difficult to reach, deliver, and afford.

01

Inadequate Infrastructure and Supply Chains

Many clinics and hospitals, particularly in rural areas, operate without beds, reliable electricity, running water, or basic surgical facilities.

Weak supply chains compound the challenge, causing chronic shortages of essential medications, vaccines, and diagnostic equipment.

02

Critical Shortage of Healthcare Workers

Sub-Saharan Africa bears over 20% of the global disease burden but houses only a tiny fraction of the world's healthcare workforce.

Limited training capacity and “brain drain” continue to deepen shortages of doctors, nurses, and midwives across the region.

03

High Out-of-Pocket Costs & Lack of Funding

Most countries in the region lack comprehensive health insurance systems, leaving patients to pay directly for medical care.

The resulting financial hardship forces many families to delay or completely skip lifesaving treatment.

Our Innovative Solution

By pairing the AI-powered "Afya Mfukoni" digital platform with zero-emission, solar-powered mobile clinics, AGKI has created a seamless "phygital" ecosystem that has already brought end-to-end medical care to over 500,000 people. Backed by a highly scalable revenue model of subscriptions, pay-per-service, and strategic partnerships, this sustainable solution bypasses traditional infrastructure to deliver quality, high-impact healthcare directly to the doorstep.
