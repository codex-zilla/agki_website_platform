{% comment %} The two inputs are written out here rather than rendered by `{{ user_form.image }}`. An `ImageField` uses Django's `ClearableFileInput`, whose own markup is a "Currently:" label, a **link to the file**, a "Clear" checkbox and a "Change:" label - which is what AGKI saw on the page, and none of which can be styled or suppressed from CSS. Rendering the two controls directly is the alternative that does not require changing `FORM_RENDERER` and adding `django.forms` to `INSTALLED_APPS` just to override one widget template. `html_name` is used rather than a typed string, and `-clear` is `ClearableFileInput`'s own suffix, so what is posted is exactly what the widget parses. {% endcomment %}
