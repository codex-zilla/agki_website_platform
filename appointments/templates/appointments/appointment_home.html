How booking works

Two steps. You ask, the doctor answers — and that settles it.

{% comment %} The page this replaces described three steps — Search, Choose, Book — and called the third "instant confirmation". Booking is a negotiation (design §0.1): the patient asks, the doctor confirms the time, the patient chooses how to meet, the doctor confirms that. Saying so here is what stops a patient reading "Request sent" as a confirmation. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} Two rounds since 2026-09-03 (plan 12.1). It was four: the doctor confirmed the time, the patient came back to choose how to meet, and the doctor confirmed that too. AGKI collapsed it — the two waits in the middle were the part patients felt. {% endcomment %}
1

You ask

Pick a doctor, a free time, and how you would like to meet: an online video call, the doctor travels to you, or you come to the laboratory. You get a booking ID.

2

The doctor answers once

They confirm it, or tell you it will not work. We email you either way, and confirming settles it — there is no second round to come back for.
